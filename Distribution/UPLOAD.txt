FINISH IN XCODE
1. Confirm your App Store Connect Splativerse record uses bundle identifier
   com.splativerse.vision and belongs to the configured developer team.
2. Open Splativerse-1.0-1.xcarchive. In Xcode Settings > Apple Accounts,
   confirm your Apple Developer account and team are available. Refresh or
   sign in again if Xcode requests it; do not change the bundle ID casually.
3. In Organizer, select the archive, choose Distribute App > App Store
   Connect, and use automatic signing. Xcode can obtain the App Store
   distribution certificate/profile with the appropriate account access.
4. Review validation results and upload when ready. After Apple processes
   the build, select version 1.0 (1) in TestFlight and configure testers.

You can export an IPA without uploading from Terminal once account access is
working, from this folder:
  xcodebuild -exportArchive -archivePath Splativerse-1.0-1.xcarchive \
    -exportPath Export -exportOptionsPlist ExportOptions.plist \
    -allowProvisioningUpdates
The export settings use destination=export, not upload.
