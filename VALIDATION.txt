Adjustable radius and density-based entry — 2026-11-14
Live detail radius: 5–60 m, 1 m slider increments, presets 5/10/20/30/60 m.
Persisted per scan; older catalogs default to 30 m. Available before entry and
while viewing. New near/far renderers share immutable splat buffers; each radius
change completes its panorama and initial near sort before atomic publication.
The overlap/refresh distance is min(5 m, radius/5). A memory-budget failure keeps
the existing view and advises leaving/re-entering with the saved radius.

Entry uses at most 65,536 position samples, rejects nearly transparent splats,
and finds the densest 4 m grid neighborhood including adjacent cells. A local
5th-percentile height estimates the floor; eye height is 1.65 m. A nearby opening
is preferred when the center appears occupied, with heading toward the center.
Return to start restores this estimate. Original camera poses are not recovered;
this heuristic does not guarantee free space or provide collision detection.
